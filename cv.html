Professional Profile

Evolutionary and computational biologist specialising in population and landscape genomics, hybridisation genetics, phylogeography, systematics and plant climate adaptation. My research combines whole-genome sequencing with reproducible bioinformatics and emerging scientific-AI methods to quantify evolutionary responses to environmental change across spatial and temporal scales. This work has been recognised by the Erich Oberdorfer Foundation Research Award (2019) and published in New Phytologist, Molecular Ecology and Critical Reviews in Plant Sciences. I aim to build an independent research programme that connects evolutionary genomics, interdisciplinary collaboration and biodiversity-informed policy.

Computational and Technical Skills

Computational Methods, AI & Data Science

  • Artificial Intelligence & Genomic Modelling: Application of AI-based Large Language Models (LLMs) for genome analysis, functional annotation, variant interpretation, and integrative bioinformatics workflows.
  • Machine Learning: Deep learning (PyTorch, TensorFlow), model development and deployment; ensemble learning frameworks (Random Forest, BRT)
  • Statistical Modelling: GLM/GAM, Bayesian inference, multivariate statistics; environmental association analyses (genome-environment relationships)
  • High-Performance Computing & Workflow Management: HPC environments, Slurm job scheduling, parallel processing, Nextflow, Snakemake
  • Programming Languages: R (Bioconductor, tidyverse), Python, Bash, Perl
  • Data Analysis & Visualization: R (ggplot2, dplyr), Python (pandas, NumPy, matplotlib, seaborn), Jupyter Notebooks

Bioinformatics, Genomics and Population Genetics

  • Genome Assembly & Annotation: De novo assembly using Illumina, ONT, PacBio HiFi and Hi-C (Flye, hifiasm, HiCanu, SPAdes, miniasm, Velvet)
  • Temporal Population Genomics: Analysis of allele‑frequency change across time points to infer selection, simulation-based outlier detection using Wright–Fisher model and Moran model frameworks (ANGSD, GATK, BCF/SAMtools, BEAGLE, PLINK)
  • Hybridisation Genomics: Introgression and gene flow analysis using ABBA‑BABA, TreeMix, and STRUCTURE/ADMIXTURE
  • Population Structure & Demographic Inference: fastsimcoal2, ∂a∂i, DIYABC, demografr; phylogenomics using RAxML, MrBayes, BEAST, SplitsTree
  • Functional & Evolutionary Genomics: Integration of conservation-based functional scores (phyloP score, GERP score, GPN) to prioritise candidate loci and link constraint with allele frequency dynamics

Landscape Ecology and Geospatial Modelling

  • Landscape Genomics: Spatial integration of genomic and environmental data; analysis of climate-driven allele frequency shifts and isolation-by-environment patterns using GIS tools (ArcGIS, QGIS) and R
  • Species Distribution Modelling (SDM): Ecological niche modelling (MaxEnt, GLM, GAM, Random Forest, BRT) to contextualize genomic variation within environmental suitability landscapes

Wet-lab Skills

  • Molecular Techniques: High-throughput DNA/RNA extraction, PCR, qPCR, gel electrophoresis, Sanger sequencing
  • Genomic Library Preparation: ddRAD-seq, Oxford Nanopore (DNA/RNA), Illumina library preparation
  • Microbiology: Aseptic technique for the isolation, culturing, and maintenance of bacteria and fungi for plant microbiome analysis and pathogen assays.
